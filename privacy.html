Privacy Policy — Jomok Subtitle Tool

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Jomok Subtitle Tool is a private, internal script operated by Dr. Jeongmok Cho to manage his own YouTube channel (Jomok-Jomok TV). It is not offered to the public and has no users other than the channel owner.

What it does

Using YouTube API Services, the tool uploads translated subtitle tracks, localized titles and descriptions, and short clips to the owner's own channel, and reads metadata of the owner's own videos.

Data collected

The tool does not collect, store, or share personal data of any YouTube user or viewer. It accesses only the owner's own channel through the owner's own Google authorization. Authorization tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer and are never shared with third parties.

Third parties

No data obtained through YouTube API Services is sold, transferred, or disclosed to anyone.

Policies

The tool uses YouTube API Services and is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the channel page: Jomok-Jomok TV on YouTube.
